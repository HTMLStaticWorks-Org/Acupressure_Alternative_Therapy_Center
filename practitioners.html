Credentials-first approach
Professional standards

Qualifications should be clear and verifiable.

Before booking, ask who will provide the session, what training they have completed, whether they hold relevant registration where applicable, and what falls within their professional scope.

  • Full name and role
  • Training provider and qualification
  • Relevant registration or membership, if applicable
  • Experience with the specific modality
  • Clear boundaries and referral practices

Your questions matter

A good practitioner welcomes questions.

They should explain the session, obtain informed consent, respect boundaries, maintain hygiene and advise you to seek medical care when symptoms are outside their scope.

Explain

Describe the method, expected sensations and evidence limits.

Respect

Ask permission and stop if you are uncomfortable.

Refer

Recognise when a concern needs medical assessment.

Before booking

Prepare for your consultation.

Have a question about training, comfort preferences or session suitability? Use these three checkpoints when speaking directly with our centre before booking.

Ask the centre

1. Verify modality-specific training

Confirm which specific practice the practitioner is trained in, the awarding institution, and whether ongoing professional development is maintained.

2. Clarify hygiene & session materials

Ask how linens, surfaces, cups or ear seeds are sanitised or replaced between visits, especially for skin-contact methods.

3. Confirm medical referral practices

A responsible practitioner asks about medications, pregnancy or recent injuries upfront and recommends medical evaluation whenever symptoms need clinical care.
